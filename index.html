IMGsome: The Visual Ribosome

[ THE METAPHOR ] In cellular architecture, a ribosome is the high-speed, non-membrane-bound engine strictly responsible for translating raw genetic instructions into structured, functional output.

This subdomain operates as the active IMGsome of the ecosystem. It is a dedicated, serverless processing body engineered to ingest heavy, unoptimized visual data and instantly translate it into lightweight, functional web assets.

Because these reactions execute entirely within the local browser memory, absolute Data Sovereignty is maintained—the raw image never leaves your machine, and the structural mutation is instantaneous.
